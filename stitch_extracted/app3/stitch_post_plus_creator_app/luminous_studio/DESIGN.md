## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an airy, precise, and human-crafted mobile studio environment. Built for creators who require both aesthetic clarity and operational focus, the interface balances modern minimalism with tactile digital craftsmanship.

The visual tone is bright, open, and confident. Rather than overwhelming the user with heavy chrome or loud decorative elements, the UI recedes into a supportive canvas of pure whites, soft alabaster tones, and calibrated slate dividers. A vivid indigo accent provides intentional guidance for focal actions, active creation states, and primary transitions.

### Key Brand Tenets
- **Airy Clarity:** Interfaces leverage expansive breathing room, allowing media assets and copy to claim priority without interface clutter.
- **Crafted Precision:** Micro-details—such as whisper-light borders, calibrated inner glows, and exact typographic hierarchy—convey professional software engineering.
- **Empowered Focus:** Accent colors are applied strictly to interactive calls to action, publishing checkpoints, and active tooling states to maintain high intentionality.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around clean white planes, cool mineral neutrals, and a resonant indigo core. 

### Surface Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity foreground panels, cards, sheets, and active canvas areas.
- **App Background (`#F8FAFC`):** The structural viewport foundation behind modular cards and navigation rails.
- **Subtle Surface Container (`#F1F5F9`):** Input fills, segmented tab tracks, avatar backdrops, and inactive pill buttons.

### Boundary & Structure
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline structure for cards, floating bars, and dividers.
- **Border Focused (`#C7D2FE`):** Soft contextual boundary used during active editing and field focus.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep charcoal/slate for maximum legibility in primary titles, numbers, and inputs.
- **Text Secondary (`#334155`):** Mid-slate for secondary headers, labels, and active navigation items.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Soft slate for timestamps, hints, metadata, and placeholder states.

### Brand Accents
- **Primary Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** Primary triggers, main publish action buttons, and active toggle fills.
- **Indigo Glow / Light Variant (`#6366F1`):** Hover/press states, selection rings, and subtle linear highlights.
- **Accent Soft Wash (`#EEF2FF`):** Tonal badge fills, selected card backdrops, and subtle state feedback.

## Typography

The typography relies on Plus Jakarta Sans across all levels to create an inviting, contemporary, and crisp mobile experience. Its geometric architecture combined with friendly, humanist curves provides high legibility on high-density displays without technical harshness.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for creator performance metrics, studio mode headers, and onboarding hero statements. Letter spacing is tightly tracked to maintain visual density.
- **Body:** Calibrated line heights (1.4x to 1.5x) prevent visual fatigue during long-form caption drafting and content planning.
- **Labels & Micro-actions:** Form labels, status tags, and action buttons employ medium-to-semibold weights with neutral or slight positive tracking to ensure rapid scanning under mobile lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a 4px/8px incremental rhythm configured for touch targets and edge safety.

### Viewport Adaptation & Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 480px):** Single-column workflow with `margin-mobile` (16px) side padding and full-width bottom creation drawers. Content cards stretch across the grid with `gutter-mobile` (12px) separation.
- **Tablet & Foldables (481px – 840px):** 2-column or dual-pane layout (inspector sidebar + content preview) utilizing standard `margin` (20px) and `gutter` (16px).
- **Desktop / Web Preview (841px+):** Fixed centered canvas container (max-width 1120px) flanked by ambient margins.

### Touch Ergonomics
Primary creation buttons and studio tool triggers must occupy a minimum interactive target of 44x44 points, surrounded by at least `space-sm` (8px) of isolation padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through a fusion of low-contrast hairline outlines and diffused ambient lighting. Harsh black drop shadows are completely absent.

### Depth Tiers
- **Flat Ground (Level 0):** Used for full app viewport canvas (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow, zero elevation.
- **Resting Surface (Level 1):** Used for feeds, feed item cards, and content blocks. 
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Outline: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`
- **Interactive Floating Layer (Level 2):** Applied to active tool selectors, bottom sheets, search overlays, and dropdown menus.
  - Background: `#FFFFFF` (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)
  - Outline: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`
- **Accent Glow Elevation (Level 3 - Indigo Triggers):** Exclusively reserved for primary floating publish triggers and primary conversion interactions.
  - Shadow: `0px 10px 20px -5px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.28), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced roundedness model (`level 2`), delivering a warm, polished, and contemporary product feel that stays structured rather than cartoonish.

### Curvature Matrix
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Checkboxes, form text inputs, small badges, and tool buttons.
- **Cards & Sheets (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem / 16px):** Content preview cards, dashboard containers, analytics tiles, and modal dialogs.
- **Drawers & Floating Navigation (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Bottom action sheets, creation toolbars, and floating camera/canvas modules.
- **Fully Rounded (`rounded-full`):** Avatars, status pills, filter chips, and circular icon triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#4F46E5` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), subtle top edge bevel, and Level 3 indigo shadow. On press: scale down to 0.98 with `#4338CA` background.
- **Secondary / Subdued:** Solid `#FFFFFF`, 1px outline of `#E2E8F0`, `#334155` text. On press: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#4F46E5` text, padding `space-sm` `space-md`. On press: `#EEF2FF`.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Container:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, radius `rounded` (8px), height 48px.
- **Text:** Input value in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#64748B` (`body-md`).
- **Focus State:** Background shifts to `#FFFFFF`, border transitions to `#6366F1` with an ambient glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Inactive:** `#F1F5F9` surface with `#334155` text, zero border, pill shape (`rounded-full`), height 32px.
- **Active:** `#EEF2FF` surface, `#4F46E5` text, 1px solid `#C7D2FE`.

### Cards & Media Panels
- **Structure:** `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `rounded-lg` (16px), Level 1 elevation.
- **Internal Padding:** `space-md` (16px) around editorial metadata; full bleed for thumbnail image/video components with 1px border separation.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Unchecked:** 20x20px square with 6px radius, border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checked:** Background `#4F46E5`, border `#4F46E5`, crisp white SVG check icon.

### Studio-Specific Components
- **Floating Tool Dock:** Elevated bottom pill floating 16px above navigation, framed in `#FFFFFF` with Level 2 elevation, housing media controls, sticker insertion, and font formatting icons.
- **Publish Progress Bar:** Dual-tone hairline track (`#E2E8F0`) with animated `#4F46E5` fill and soft trailing gradient blur.
